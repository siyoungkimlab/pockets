Finding ligand pockets in apo structures

For each pair: the apo and holo structures; the sites Schrödinger MxMD + SiteMap finds; and the pockets CRUMB finds in coarse-grained simulations of the apo protein, with Martini 3, SIRAH and Martini 2. Every site and pocket comes with the MD frame it was found in, everything superposed on the holo structure.

Pick a site or pocket in the list on the right of a pair's page: its frame comes with it, and the box in the corner gives its distance to the ligand, how much of the ligand it covers, and how much of it the ligand fills. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, click an atom to see what it is; SiteView looks at the ligand's pocket, WholeView looks at everything. Pocket RMSD: apo against holo, heavy atoms of the residues within 5 Å of the ligand. Burial: the share of the ligand's surface the holo protein covers.
